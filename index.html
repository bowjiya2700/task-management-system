Help & FAQ

This application helps you create, organize, track, and manage your daily tasks.

Go to My Task, click Create Task, enter the required details, and click Save Task.

A Vital Task is an important or time-sensitive task that you want to keep separate from your regular tasks.

Click the Edit button on the task you want to modify, update the information, and save it.

Not Started means the task hasn't begun. In Progress means you are currently working on it. Completed means the task has been finished.
